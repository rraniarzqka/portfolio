The Challenge

Boleh Belajar wanted to drive qualified traffic to its Performance Marketing Bootcamp enrollment page through a Google Search Ads campaign.

The campaign ran for seven days on a Rp30,000 daily budget while competing against larger, better-funded advertisers.

With a limited budget, success depended on campaign structure, keyword selection, and ad relevance.

The Approach

The work focused on three areas.

1

Keyword Group Test

Split the campaign into Product, Competitor, and Generic keyword groups, with one tailored ad for each to compare performance across different search intents.

2

Mid-Campaign Changes

After 48 hours, the Competitor group underperformed and was paused. Its budget was moved to a new Generic keyword group targeting marketing education terms, which became the campaign's best performer.

3

Matching the Message to the Audience

Tested different messages across audience groups. Career-focused copy performed best with younger users, while the Generic group attracted an older audience, helping shape recommendations for the next campaign.
