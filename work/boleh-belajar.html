Snapshot

The Challenge

Drive bootcamp enrollment traffic on Rp 30K/day against well-funded competitors.

The Strategy

A three-way ad-group test with a fast mid-flight restructure.

The Outcome

10.17% campaign CTR — 2.5× the target.

The brief

Boleh Belajar is an Indonesian edtech platform running career bootcamps. The task: drive qualified traffic to the Performance Marketing Bootcamp (Batch 22) enrollment page with a Google Ads search campaign — on a deliberately tight budget of Rp 30,000 a day for seven days, in a keyword auction crowded with well-funded competitors like RevoU, Purwadhika, and MySkill.

The real test: with no budget to buy volume, every rupiah had to go to the right query — making structure, keyword selection, and ad relevance the entire game.

The approach

A structured test-and-optimize sprint:

1

Three-way ad-group test

Split the budget across Product keywords (bootcamp/course terms), Competitor keywords (rival brand names), and Generic keywords (learning-intent terms) — all phrase match, one tailored ad each, so performance differences meant something.

2

Mid-flight restructure — kill the loser fast

After 48 hours the Competitor group had 107 impressions, 5 clicks, and low quality scores. Paused it on day 3 and reallocated its slot to a fresh Generic ad group targeting "belajar marketing" and "pelatihan digital marketing" — which became the campaign's best performer.

3

Message-audience fit

Career-angle copy ("upgrade your career") won for the younger Product audience (25–34), while the Generic group skewed 45–54 — surfacing a demographic split that shaped the copy recommendations for the next flight.

Execution highlights

🏆

The career angle wins

The winning Generic ad hit 11.17% CTR with an "Excellent" Google ad-strength rating; the "from zero" beginner angle held a stable 10.63% on Product terms.

🎯

One keyword at 31% CTR

"Bootcamp digital marketing" clicked at 31.03% — nearly one in three people who saw the ad — flagging it as the scale-up keyword for any budget increase.

🔗

Sitelinks that sell

The "Career & Internship Package" sitelink drew 52 clicks at 9.83% CTR — the offer, not just the ad, was doing the persuading.

⚖️

Honest about the misses

CPC landed at Rp 2,417 vs. the Rp 1,000 benchmark — the cost of competing in a premium auction on a micro budget. The report says so plainly, with bid-schedule and keyword-pruning fixes attached.

What's next

The optimization plan is written and ready: scale budget behind the 31%-CTR keyword, lower bids on the budget-eating "belajar marketing" term, add day-of-week bid adjustments (Product converts early week, Generic mid-to-late), and split ad copy by demographic — career language for 25–34, business-digitalization language for 45–54.
