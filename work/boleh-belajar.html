The Challenge

Boleh Belajar wanted to drive qualified traffic to its Performance Marketing Bootcamp enrollment page through a Google Search Ads campaign.

The campaign ran for seven days on a Rp30,000 daily budget while competing against larger, better-funded advertisers.

With a limited budget, success depended on campaign structure, keyword selection, and ad relevance.

The Approach

The work focused on three areas.

1

Three-Way Keyword Test

Split the campaign into Product, Competitor, and Generic keyword groups, with one tailored ad for each to compare performance across different search intents.

2

Fast Mid-Campaign Optimization

After 48 hours, the Competitor group underperformed and was paused. Its budget was reallocated to a new Generic keyword group targeting marketing education terms, which became the campaign's best performer.

3

Message–Audience Fit

Tested different messaging across audience groups. Career-focused copy performed best with younger users, while the Generic group attracted an older audience, helping shape recommendations for the next campaign.

What's next

The optimization plan is written and ready: scale budget behind the 31%-CTR keyword, lower bids on the budget-eating "belajar marketing" term, add day-of-week bid adjustments (Product converts early week, Generic mid-to-late), and split ad copy by demographic — career language for 25–34, business-digitalization language for 45–54.
