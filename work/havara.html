Havara Studio · D2C Leather Goods

197 leads in 7 days for a handmade leather brand

197

new WhatsApp leads in one week — at 64% below benchmark cost per lead

RolePerformance marketer (Meta Ads)
Timeframe7-day sprint · Jun–Jul 2026
ScopeFull-funnel Meta Ads · Creative strategy · Reporting

Where they were stuck

Havara Studio makes genuine-leather bags with timeless, practical designs — handmade, pre-order, sold entirely through WhatsApp and Instagram DMs. No e-commerce. The target market is career women aged 20–45 who need a capable everyday bag; the actual buyers skewed boomer, and the core segment simply didn't know the brand existed.

The brief: build awareness in the right audience and prove that paid ads could fill the WhatsApp inbox with real buying conversations — on a total budget under Rp 1 million for the week.

The approach

A full-funnel Meta Ads structure, with KPI benchmarks agreed before a single rupiah was spent:

1

ToFU — Awareness (20% of budget)

Reach and brand-awareness objective, creative built on the USPs: genuine leather, timeless design, custom name engraving. Benchmark: CPM Rp 15–35K, reach 15–40K people.

2

MoFU — Traffic to conversation (40% of budget)

WhatsApp messaging objective with education-and-lifestyle creative: material details, compartments, durability — the questions buyers actually DM about. Benchmark: CPL Rp 10–25K.

3

BoFU — Sales conversations (40% of budget)

Conversion via WhatsApp and Instagram DM with urgency-and-offer angles: limited stock, free shipping, chat with the admin. Attribution: 7-day click / 1-day view.

Execution highlights

📣

Awareness at 3.4× the target

135,229 people reached against a 15–40K target, at a CPM of Rp 1,993 — nearly a tenth of the benchmark ceiling. The awareness problem got cheaper to solve than anyone budgeted for.

💬

WhatsApp as the conversion event

For a handmade pre-order business, the honest KPI is a new buying conversation, not a checkout. Both mid- and bottom-funnel campaigns were optimized to New Messaging Contacts.

🔎

The age-gap insight

Awareness reach skewed 25–34, but the people who actually started chats were 35–54. That mismatch became the key recommendation: shift MoFU/BoFU targeting toward 35–54.

📅

Midweek is money

Leads peaked Wednesday–Thursday (31/day) and dipped on weekends — a free scheduling optimization for every future flight of ads.

Results

197 new WhatsApp/DM leads in 7 days
Rp 3.6K cost per lead — 64% under benchmark
3.4× the awareness reach target, at 1/10th benchmark CPM

New leads per day, 28 Jun – 4 Jul 2026

Source: Meta Ads Manager. Peak of 31 leads/day on Wed–Thu; 179 leads from Traffic + Sales campaigns, 197 total including awareness spillover.

What's next

The honest gap: leads are tracked, closings aren't yet. Next flight adds WhatsApp Business labels to measure lead-to-order rate (and true ROAS), scales the proven Traffic and Sales budgets, retargets the engaged audience from this flight, and builds 1–3% lookalikes from it — expansion without losing relevance.
