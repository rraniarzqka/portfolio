Havara Studio · D2C Leather Goods

197 WhatsApp leads in 7 days for a handmade leather brand

Snapshot

The Challenge

Havara Studio was still relatively unknown to its target audience, making it harder to reach the right people and turn interest into WhatsApp conversations.

The Strategy

A full-funnel Meta Ads campaign that moved people from awareness to conversation to purchase.

The Outcome

197 WhatsApp leads in 7 days at 64% below the benchmark cost per lead.

The Challenge

Havara Studio makes handmade genuine-leather bags with practical, timeless designs. Made to order and sold entirely through WhatsApp and Instagram DMs, the brand had no e-commerce store. Its target audience was career women aged 20–45, but existing customers skewed older and awareness among the target segment remained low.

The goal was to reach more of the right audience and generate real buying conversations through WhatsApp, with a total weekly budget of under Rp1 million.

The Approach

I built a three-stage Meta Ads funnel, with the budget and performance benchmarks set before launch.

1

Awareness · 20% of budget

Reach and brand awareness campaigns focused on Havara Studio's key USPs: genuine leather, timeless design, and custom name engraving.

Benchmark: CPM Rp5–10K · Reach 15–40K people

2

Consideration · 40% of budget

WhatsApp campaigns designed to turn product interest into conversations, using creative that answered common questions about materials, compartments, and durability.

Benchmark: CPL Rp10–25K

3

Sales Conversations · 40% of budget

WhatsApp and Instagram DM campaigns focused on people closer to buying, using stronger purchase-intent angles such as limited stock and free shipping.

Execution Highlights

📣

Awareness Above Target

Reached 135,229 people against a 15–40K target, with a Rp1,993 CPM.

💬

WhatsApp as the Conversion Point

Optimized mid- and bottom-funnel campaigns for new messaging contacts, turning interest into buying conversations.

🔎

Audience Insight

Awareness reach skewed 25–34, while new conversations came mainly from 35–54. This helped identify a stronger audience for future lead campaigns.

📅

Midweek Performance

Leads peaked on Wednesday and Thursday, reaching 31 per day, while weekends performed lower.
