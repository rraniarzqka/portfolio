The Challenge

The Outcome

AI Overview presence up from 36% to 77% of opportunities.

The Challenge

PRENAGEN generated over 41 million monthly search impressions across pregnancy and parenting topics. However, by late 2025, impressions continued to grow while organic clicks declined as AI Overviews began providing instant answers directly on the search results page, reducing the need for users to click through.

A technical audit found that much of the site's evergreen medical content was still using NewsArticle schema, limiting its visibility in AI Overviews and other AI-powered search experiences.

The Approach

A content-led engagement with technical and AI-search workstreams:

1

Technical SEO & Structured Data

Improved the site's technical foundation by updating structured data from NewsArticle to BlogPosting and implementing MedicalSymptom schema where relevant. We also fixed server errors, repaired broken redirects, and improved mobile performance through WebP image optimization and lazy loading.

2

Content Strategy & Optimization

Built a monthly SEO content pipeline based on search demand, refreshed underperforming articles, and expanded high-performing topic clusters. Existing content was updated to improve rankings, while new content targeted emerging search opportunities and product-related intent.

3

AI Search Optimization (GEO/AEO)

Optimized content for AI-powered search by creating structured FAQ content and improving page organization for better discoverability in AI Overviews and other generative search platforms. We also promoted interactive tools that encouraged users to visit the site instead of relying solely on AI-generated answers.

What's next

The roadmap doubles down on trust signals: an editorial-team initiative pairing verified medical credentials with reviewer schema and expert-led layouts, symptom-to-content schema mapping so AI engines recommend PRENAGEN for symptom searches, and closing the visibility gap in conversational AI (Gemini, ChatGPT) to match the ~70% citation rates already won in Perplexity and AI Overviews.
