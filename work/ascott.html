The Ascott Limited · DiscoverASR · Global Hospitality

From page-2 brand to page-1 authority, worldwide

20.5 → 8.9

average Google position across the platform, in 12 months

RoleSEO & Content Team
Timeframe2025 – 2026 · Ongoing
ScopeTechnical SEO · Content · AI Search (GEO)

The Challenge

DiscoverASR captured nearly 90% of branded search demand but appeared in only 18% of high-intent generic searches such as "serviced apartments in Singapore". Most non-branded traffic was captured by OTAs and competitors.

Visibility was also limited by page-two rankings for high-value keywords, technical SEO issues including broken structured data, and the growing presence of Google AI Overviews in research-driven searches.

The Approach

The work focused on three areas:

1

Technical SEO

Resolved issues affecting rendering, structured data, and indexation while prioritizing keywords ranking in positions 11–20. During this period, the domain's Authority Score increased from 45 to 65.

2

Content Strategy

Created destination guides, persona-led content, and hyper-local travel insights to capture non-branded search demand. Content focused on providing detailed, destination-specific information that users may not find in AI-generated summaries.

3

AI Search Visibility

Optimized content for AI-driven search experiences, increasing Google AI Overview citations from 301 to 825 in one quarter. Total AI mentions reached 8.2K, while brand favorability exceeded 70% across ChatGPT and Gemini.

Execution Highlights

🧗

First-Page Keyword Optimization

Systematically moved keywords ranking in positions 11–20 onto page one through metadata optimization, internal linking, and content expansion, helping improve average rankings from 20.5 to 8.9.

🌏

Market-Specific Strategy

Prioritized growth markets such as Thailand, Indonesia, and Australia while rebuilding visibility in mature markets including Singapore and Japan, ensuring resources were focused on areas with the greatest search opportunity.

🤖

Content for AI Search Gaps

Created content around travel questions that AI-generated summaries may not fully address, such as Seoul winter stays with ondol heating and serviced apartment safety features, helping capture users seeking more detailed information.

🛒

Conversion Funnel Optimization

Improved the booking journey with FAQ schema, price-focused snippets, and high-intent property content targeting users closer to purchase. These enhancements increased the checkout-to-purchase rate from 62.6% to 69.4%.

What's next

The 2026 roadmap focuses on reclaiming clicks from AI Overviews with CTR work (sharper titles, FAQ schema, AI-mode-ready snippets), stabilizing Citadines' visibility in Singapore and Tokyo, and continuing the booking-journey improvements that lifted checkout-to-purchase from 62.6% to 69.4%.
