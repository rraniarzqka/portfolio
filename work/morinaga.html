+95.6% sessions in one month; clicks 3.1× since October.

The Challenge

By late 2025, Morinaga.id's impressions were growing while organic clicks declined as AI-generated search results became more prominent. Older articles were also losing ranking queries due to outdated information, weak structure, and declining relevance.

As competitors such as Enfa and Bebelac continued refreshing their content, Morinaga needed to systematically improve its existing content while finding new opportunities for organic growth.

The Approach

The work focused on three areas:

1

Content Refresh

Refreshed underperforming articles based on declining traffic, outdated information, and content gaps. Ranking queries grew by 20%+ across refreshed content.

2

Topic Strategy

Built a stronger content cluster around "dongeng sebelum tidur", helping one refreshed article grow 275.8% to 59,015 monthly clicks and contribute roughly a quarter of total organic traffic.

3

AI Search Visibility

Restructured content for AI-powered search with answer-first formats, clearer lists, and structured FAQs. Morinaga reached an average AI search position of 2.6, with AI Overview visibility peaking at 2,200 keywords.
