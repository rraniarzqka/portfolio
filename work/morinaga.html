The Challenge

By late 2025, Morinaga.id's impressions were growing while organic clicks declined as AI-generated search results became more prominent. Older articles were also losing ranking queries due to outdated information, weak structure, and declining relevance.

As competitors such as Enfa and Bebelac continued refreshing their content, Morinaga needed to systematically improve its existing content while finding new opportunities for organic growth.

The Approach

The work focused on three areas:

1

Content Refresh

Identified underperforming articles based on declining traffic, outdated information, weak structure, and incomplete answers. These articles were refreshed and optimized to better match current search intent. The refreshed content saw strong growth in clicks and impressions, with ranking queries increasing by more than 20%.

2

Topic Strategy

Built a stronger content cluster around "dongeng sebelum tidur", a high-demand topic among Indonesian parents. By expanding and optimizing content around the topic, Morinaga grew its visibility across the cluster. One refreshed article grew 275.8% to 59,015 clicks in a single month, eventually contributing roughly a quarter of total organic traffic.

3

AI Search Visibility

Optimized content structure for AI-powered search through answer-first formats, clearer lists, and structured FAQ content. This helped achieve an average AI search position of 2.6, with Google AI Overview visibility peaking at 2,200 keywords in February 2026.

Execution Highlights

📚

Content Refresh at Scale

Systematically identified and refreshed underperforming articles, focusing on content accuracy, structure, and search intent. The optimized content saw growth in clicks, impressions, and ranking queries.

📈

High-Demand Topic Visibility

Morinaga built strong visibility around the "bedtime stories" cluster. In June 2026, a single query generated 48,844 clicks, with the leading article accounting for 24.5% of total organic traffic.

🤖

AI Search Visibility

Structured content for AI-powered search, helping Morinaga reach 2,200 keywords in Google AI Overviews in February 2026, its highest recorded level, while maintaining visibility across approximately 2,194 keywords in March.

📅

Seasonal Content Planning

Aligned content updates and new content pushes with seasonal search demand, including the December spike in bedtime-story searches and post-holiday changes in search behavior.
