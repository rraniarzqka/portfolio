The Outcome

545K monthly organic visits — the market #1 in Indonesian wildlife tourism.

The Challenge

Taman Safari Indonesia operates six destinations: Taman Safari Bogor, Prigen, Bali Safari & Marine Park, Jakarta Aquarium, Solo Safari, and Varuna. Each park competes for family travel and day-trip searches alongside zoos, aquariums, and travel OTAs.

The challenge was to build visibility across the portfolio while search behavior continued to expand beyond traditional Google results into Google Discover and AI-powered search.

The Approach

The work focused on three areas:

1

Wildlife Content

Built wildlife-focused content around questions families actively search for, from animal facts to species-specific topics such as elephant diets and Komodo dragons. This created new opportunities to introduce users to Taman Safari's parks and attractions. The content helped generate 1,520 keywords in Google's top 3 and 1,196 Google AI Overview appearances.

2

Ticket & Park Search

Optimized ticket, promotional, and "things to do" pages across the portfolio for commercial-intent searches. The portfolio maintained an average position of 5.3 across a broad keyword set, while December clicks increased 32.25% month over month during the holiday period.

3

Google Discover

Developed story-led content to reach new audiences through Google Discover. A single article generated 32,854 clicks and helped drive organic traffic growth during the peak holiday period.

Execution Highlights

🔗

Portfolio-Wide Internal Linking

Connected the six park websites through strategic internal linking to strengthen relationships between destinations, attractions, and wildlife content across the portfolio. Domain Authority increased from 46 to 54.

📰

Discover-Led Content

Developed story-led content around seasonal interests and trending topics to reach new audiences through Google Discover. The strongest-performing article generated 32,854 clicks.

🎟️

Seasonal Search Planning

Aligned content and optimization efforts with periods of high travel demand, contributing to +32% clicks in December and +36% in March.

🤖

AI Search Visibility

Wildlife and family-travel content earned 1,196 Google AI Overview appearances alongside 1,520 top-3 keywords, keeping Taman Safari visible as search expands into AI-generated results.
