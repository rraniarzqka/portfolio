Where they were stuck

SOZO Skin is an aesthetic-clinic brand under Seven Retail Group. Its website had the classic clinic-site problem: treatment pages that ranked poorly for the searches patients actually make ("laser wajah", treatment prices, before/after questions), thin supporting content, and landing pages that lost visitors quickly once they arrived.

The brief: grow qualified organic traffic to the treatment pages — the pages that turn readers into consultation bookings — with on-page work and content, in a single quarter.

The approach

A hands-on, fundamentals-first SEO sprint:

1

Keyword mapping & on-page optimization

Mapped every treatment page to the queries patients use, then rewrote titles, metas, headings, and internal links to match. Keywords in the top 3 grew +47%, and first-page keywords +23%, within the quarter.

2

Landing-page revamp

Restructured underperforming treatment landing pages — clearer answers above the fold, visible pricing cues, and consultation CTAs. Bounce rate on the optimized pages dropped from 19.82% to 14.71%.

3

Content that compounds

Published skincare-education articles targeting question queries; the new content lifted sessions +11.18% within its first 30 days, and ten articles earned placements in Google Discover for bursts of extra reach.

Execution highlights

🗺️

Patient-language keyword map

Built the keyword set from how patients actually phrase concerns — symptoms and goals, not clinic jargon — so pages met searchers where they start.

🩺

Treatment pages that answer first

Each revamped page leads with the question the visitor came with (what it treats, how it feels, what it costs) before the sales pitch — the bounce-rate drop followed directly.

📱

Ten articles into Discover

Story-style education content with strong imagery repeatedly surfaced in Google Discover — outsized reach for a clinic-sized content budget.

⚡

Fast feedback loops

Weekly Search Console reviews decided what got optimized next — effort always went to the page closest to a ranking breakthrough.
